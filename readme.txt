
How to Download and Install the game plus how to play the mod:

First how to download.
Make a local copy of this branch onto your pc. Inside this branch there is a folder named zombies, it holds the dll of the mod.
With this folder you will go to your quake 2 folder inside steam and inside quake 2 you will drag and drop this zombies folder into quake 2.
After that is done, right click on quake 2 and make a shortcut of quake 2.
With this short cut you will right click it and go to properties. 
In properties you will go to the end of the target section and add "+ set game Zombies" to the end of the line.
This should now let you play the mod.

How to play:

For the mod, you will be facing off hordes of zombies. Around the map there will be npc's that can sell you colas, sell you a weapon at random, or upgrade your weapon to its better half. Your goal is to survive as long as possible. You activate this mod by pressing = sign. Or press tab and it'll tell you what to do.
